# Island shelf rules (from the user)
- Two prompts per person (clothed, studio/nude), plus a third swimwear/underwear prompt when the roster has one.
- Core appearance block is identical in every prompt for a person (name, age, people incl. "East-Asian looking" etc., height, build, bust cup for women only, skin, face, eyes, hair, marks). Men's Scale never appears.
- Prompt age is never below 22 (roster ages 19 to 21 are written as 22 in prompts only).
- Framing: "three-quarter length shot framed from the head down to the knees, knees included, hips and thighs fully in view" (never "full body").
- Poses are body-only: no objects that need anything beyond the plain background. Vary them (shy, bold, relaxed). Nude poses must not hide the anatomy (no crossed/wrapped arms on women, no hands clasped in front on men).
- Studio (nude) prompts end, after the background, with: women "nipples and vagina visible"; men "average size penis", unless the roster note states a size ("larger than average" -> "larger than average penis", "huge" -> "huge penis").
- UI label for nude is "Studio". Nude and swimwear prompts are shown by default, with tick boxes to hide them.
- Build: python3 -I build_island.py ROSTER.csv OUT.json poses_<island>; then assemble.py with "key|Name|file.json" per island.
- Relatives of player characters: the roster note names the character they resemble (e.g. "Duncan's colouring"). Prompts never contain those names. Keep the shared features themselves (colouring, brows, grin, glasses, beauty spot, cheekbones) as plain description. Corrected rows live in Relatives_Fixed_Rows.csv; merge_fixed.py swaps them into the island rosters (with names stripped) before build_island.py runs.
- Species: see SPECIES.md. species.py adds each species' appearance to the core block of every one of that person's prompts (wolf-kin ears/tail/ruff, cat-kin patched coat, frost-folk frost patterns, ember-folk lava veins and so on). Ghost folk never get studio prompts.
- Third (swimwear/underwear) prompt: ONLY for people who are in, or could turn up in, a place where swimwear or bathwear is worn (beaches, baths, spas, pools, springs, sea caves, changing huts, wash and scrub houses, massage rooms; swimmers, lifeguards, bathers). Everyone else gets two prompts only (clothed + studio), even if the roster fills in the third-image column. Enforced by wants_third() in build_island.py.
- Adult-venue workers and people who sell themselves (madams, hostesses, companions, company workers, hustlers, brothel/parlour/tent/salon/lodging-house staff, alley and street folk, homeless) get ONE non-nude prompt, not a clothed one plus a third: their normal attire is lingerie, drawers, a slutty dress or ragged torn underwear, so the roster's underwear item becomes their "clothed" outfit. Two prompts total (that + studio). Enforced by is_adult_worker() in build_island.py.
- **STANDING RULE (user): adult workers are NEVER made with a normal clothed prompt, and NEVER get a third prompt, whatever the roster's columns say (even if a roster says "make 3 images").** They always get exactly two prompts: (1) their normal attire (the roster's underwear item, or a default from adult_default() in build_island.py if the roster gives none: lace/silk lingerie for venue staff, ragged/patched/torn for street, selling-for-coin and homeless people; drawers or boxers for men) and (2) the studio prompt. When a new roster arrives, also check by eye for adult workers the keyword rules might miss and add them.
