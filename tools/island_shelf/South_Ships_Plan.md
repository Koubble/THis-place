# South Ships Plan (draft for corrections)
14 ordinary ships (7 tiny, 5 small, 2 medium) plus 2 ghost ships. 44 ordinary crew plus 10 ghosts = 54 people. No ship bigger than medium. Encounters are interactions only (talk, trade, a threat, a favour asked, a challenge): no carrying, hiring, messages or jobs. The South has the most mixed peoples: ogres, Amazons, ember folk and cat-kin as well as humans. Looks: sun-browned, bright cloth, rum and salt, tropical heat; patched sails on the poorer boats.

## Tiny ships (2 crew each)
1. The Pearl-Diver Dinghy: Nacre Atoll. Woman 30 and man 24, lean and tanned. Harmless; sell pearls and shell beads, talk about holding your breath.
2. The Rum Runner Skiff: Molasse. Man 40 and woman 35, mildly drunk. Harmless; offer a drink, sell cheap rum, grumble about the planters.
3. The Healer's Boat: Greenhallow. Woman 45 and man 38, calm, with a trace of a northern lilt. Harmless; sell herbs and salves.
4. The Hustlers' Boat: Tarnsand, patched and rank. Man 32 and woman 27, in cheap finery. Suggestive and grabby; sell a quick, seedy thrill and keep pushing, but take a no.
5. The Mask Skiff: Masquelle. Man 29 and woman 26, masked and tipsy. Flirty and teasing; drape garlands on you and pull you into a dance, harmless but forward.
6. The Treasure Hunters' Rowboat: Ixmara. Man 46 and woman 37, jumpy and mud-stained. Paranoid; sell odd old trinkets and warn you off the ruins.
7. The Cutlass Lookout Skiff: Cutlass Bay. Man 35 and man 28, sly. A threat: scouting for pirates, they eye your cargo and demand a "toll" if you look soft.

## Small ships (3 to 4 crew)
8. The Pirate Sloop: Cutlass Bay. 4 crew, mixed (man 41, woman 36, man 28, woman 33). A threat: demand loot, then settle for a bribe or a good story; a strong party gets a grudging trade.
9. The Amazon Cutter: Maenara. 4 women, ages 30 to 45, all Amazons, no men aboard. Haughty and tough; warm to women, scathing or threatening to any man in the party.
10. The Bargewater Trader: 3 crew, mixed (woman 50, man 44, man 25). Fair dealers; trade and quote prices.
11. The Ember Trading Boat: Emberfall. 4 ember folk, mixed (men 38 and 45, women 31 and 40), soot-stained, glowing veins. Proud and a touch hot-tempered; sell metalwork and tools.
12. The Cat-Kin Skiff: Calico traders in the south. 3 cat-kin, mixed (woman 24, man 28, woman 32). Curious and flirty; poke through your gear and tease you, harmless.

## Medium ships (5 to 6 crew)
13. The Ogre Raider: Thornmaw. 6 ogres, mixed (men 44, 38, 35, 30, women 41, 33), huge and brutish. Up to no good: demand a toll in goods, shove past a weak party and threaten, brutal but not suicidal; a strong party gets a rough bargain.
14. The Planters' Brig: Molasse. 6 crew, mixed (planter man 55, overseer woman 44, men 38 and 31, women 29 and 26). Entitled and bossy; bark orders at the party, drink on deck, and the two younger crew are lewd and forward.

## Ghost ships (South; haunting ground Thornmaw, Maenara, Ixmara)
15. The Old Purity Ship (medium, 6): ghost captain man 55, first mate woman 42, preacher man 48, sailors man 30 and woman 26, cook woman 52. All human, from an age when humans held themselves above the other peoples and were sunk by them. They ignore or insult any non-human aboard and warm only to an all-human party.
16. The Drowned Pearl-Divers (small, 4): men 40 and 25, women 36 and 23, lost in a squall. Sad and gentle; bargain pearls and ask the way home.

## Questions with defaults
1. Share: about 4 trouble ships (7, 8, 13, and the Amazon cutter 9 for men), 4 grabby or suggestive (4, 5, 14, and 12 gently), 6 harmless, 2 ghosts. Default yes.
2. Non-human crews: ogres, Amazons, ember folk and cat-kin, 17 of 44. Default yes.
3. Ship names: I give each a distinct working name when I build. Default yes.
