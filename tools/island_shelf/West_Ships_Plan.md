# West Ships Plan (draft for corrections)
14 ordinary ships (7 tiny, 5 small, 2 medium) plus 2 ghost ships. 43 ordinary crew plus 9 ghosts = 52 people. No ship bigger than medium. Encounters are interactions only (talk, trade, a threat, a favour asked, a challenge): no carrying, hiring, messages or jobs. Mostly human, with cat-kin and stone-folk aboard one ship each. The West is the least heavy region, so fewer ships here are predatory. Looks: wool and oilskin, wind-reddened, farm and timber and iron on the work boats; the Murkfen and Gallows boats are grey and damp.

## Tiny ships (2 crew each)
1. The Fishers' Pair: two women 40 and 33, plain-spoken fishers. Harmless; sell fish and gossip about the weather.
2. The Wine Skiff: Vinemarch. Man 52 and woman 28, flushed from tasting. Harmless; offer a taste and sell a bottle.
3. The Test Hull: Keelhaven. Shipwright man 36 and woman 30 trying out a new hull. Proud and harmless; ask your opinion on the lines.
4. The Timber Raft Tug: Timberfell. Men 34 and 41, big and bearded. Gruff and harmless; challenge a party member to an arm-wrestle or a drink-off.
5. The Highland Coracle: Cairnmoor. Man 45 and woman 38, wary in tartan. Harmless; sell a dram and say little.
6. The Prison Tender Skiff: Gallows Rock. Warder man 40 and woman 33, grey and hard. A threat: they stop you, search your gear and demand a "fine".
7. The Fen Peddlers' Boat: Murkfen. Man 38 and woman 44, pale and damp. A threat of the quiet kind: they press goods on you "on credit", then claim you owe them and lean on you until you settle.

## Small ships (3 to 4 crew)
8. The Cider Boat: Harrowfield. 3 crew, mixed (woman 55, man 27, woman 24), sunny and sticky. Cheerful and harmless; sell cider, and the younger two flirt openly.
9. The Calico Rope Boat: 3 cat-kin, mixed (woman 26, man 30, woman 34), curious and flirty. Tease, paw at your gear and invite a game; harmless.
10. The Iron Barge: Steelmarch. 4 crew, mixed (men 42 and 36, women 39 and 28), sooty and strong. Rough but fair; trade iron goods and mock your boat.
11. The Quarry Barge: Stonewake. 3 crew, two stone-folk (man and woman) and a human scholar man 41. Slow and polite; trade stone and talk about old ruins.
12. The Gentleman's Launch: Oldgate. 4 crew, mixed (old-money man 58, women 27 and 25, man 24). The old man is rich, bored and probing; he wants to pay for one of the party's time on an evening, and his companions are suggestive. He takes a no.

## Medium ships (5 to 6 crew)
13. The Timber Brig: Timberfell. 6 crew, mixed (men 40, 35 and 29; women 38, 31 and 26), huge and cheerful. Rowdy; demand a drink-off over a barrel, loud and flirty, and take a loss with a roar.
14. The Labour Broker's Barge: Murkfen. 6 crew, mixed (gangmaster man 49, ledger-keeper woman 46, heavies men 38 and 33 and women 35 and 28). A threat: shake the party down, wave a ledger, claim a debt from a "friend" of the party and offer a one-sided deal. Contracts and debts only, never people.

## Ghost ships (West; haunting ground Gallows Rock, Murkfen, Stonewake)
15. The Convict Hulk (medium, 6): ghost warder man 50 and convicts woman 34, man 29, woman 41, man 38, woman 25. Died chained and wanting off. Lonely and desperate; they plead and cling and trail after the party rather than attack.
16. The Fen Ferry (small, 3): ghost ferrymen man 60 and man 45 and a fen-wife woman 52 who keeps the tally. They ask for a debt in coin, and they are polite and relentless.

## Questions with defaults
1. Share: about 3 trouble ships (6, 7, 14), 3 grabby or suggestive (8, 9, 12; the cat-kin only tease), 8 harmless, 2 ghosts. Default yes.
2. Non-human crews: cat-kin on ship 9, stone-folk on ship 11. Default yes.
3. Ship names: I give each a distinct working name when I build. Default yes.
