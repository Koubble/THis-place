# East Ships Plan (draft for corrections)
14 ordinary ships (7 tiny, 5 small, 2 medium) plus 2 ghost ships. 44 ordinary crew plus 9 ghosts = 53 people. No ship bigger than medium. Encounters are interactions only (talk, trade, a threat, a favour asked, a challenge): no carrying, hiring, messages or jobs. Mostly human, with scalekin on the salt barge. Looks: rich and polished, silks and brass, sun-glare and salt; the poorer boats are patched. The East leans seedy, so more ships here are suggestive or predatory than in the North.

## Tiny ships (2 crew each)
1. The Regatta Skiff: man 27 and woman 25, young racing sailors in good kit. Harmless; boast, bet small coins and show off.
2. The Admiralty Launch: petty officer man 38 and woman 33 from Brinewatch, strict. Harmless if you are tidy; they check your flag and lecture you.
3. The Opera Lighter: stagehands man 45 and woman 30 hauling scenery to Lyrenne. Harmless and gossipy; trade rumours about the stars.
4. The Rent-Collector's Boat: agents man 44 and man 36 for an absentee landlord. A threat: they demand "dues" from anyone they decide is a tenant and lean on a weak party.
5. The Masked Ferry: a Duskmarket night boat, woman 40 and man 29 in masks. Odd and a little creepy; sell masks and strange trinkets, never remove the masks.
6. The Courtesan Skiff: a Rosewater host man 28 and hostess woman 31, in silk. Suggestive and forward; offer an intimate evening for a fee and keep asking, but take a no.
7. The Loan Shark's Dinghy: Dicehold. Man 50, heavy-set, and woman 35, muscular. A threat: they offer a loan on ruinous terms and make menacing noises when you refuse.

## Small ships (3 to 4 crew)
8. The Racing Yacht: 4 crew, mixed (men 26 and 31, women 24 and 29), rich and glossy. Harmless; challenge you to a sporting wager.
9. The Merchant Cutter: 3 crew from Goldspire, mixed (woman 49, man 42, man 27). Fair dealers; trade goods and quote prices.
10. The Gambling Barge: 4 crew, mixed (woman 46, men 39 and 33, woman 28), Dicehold tables afloat. A cheat: loaded dice and marked cards; fleece you if you play, friendly if you do not.
11. The Pleasure Barge: 4 crew, mixed (women 34 and 28, men 31 and 26), a floating Rosewater party. Grabby and suggestive; they press an intimate afternoon on one of the party and get pushy with drink.
12. The Salt Barge: 3 crew from Saltglare, two scalekin (man and woman, both about 40) and a human man 36. Gruff and harmless; sell salt, talk about the glare.

## Medium ships (5 to 6 crew)
13. The Veilhollow Pilgrim Ship: 6 devotees, mixed (leader woman 52, men 33 and 29, women 36, 26, 41), robed and fervent. Pushy: preach, ask for a donation, and urge you to join a rite on the spot; they chant and crowd you if you refuse but do not attack.
14. The Gala Yacht: 6 crew and guests from Goldspire (financier man 58, his wife woman 50, a politician man 45, an entertainer woman 30, a steward man 33, a maid woman 27). Entitled and condescending; the entertainer and the steward are flirty, and the financier tries to buy your silence over something he saw.

## Ghost ships (East; haunting ground Saltglare, Veilhollow, Roarhold)
15. The Old Slave Ship (medium, 6): ghost captain man 54, mate man 43, overseer woman 40, and three ghost captives bound for the pit games (female champion 28, male fighter 31, older man 50). The captors keep up the old trade and bargain with the party; the captives plead, and breaking the old chain ends the haunting. Past, not present, and shown as a theme.
16. The Ghost Dice-Boat (small, 3): dealer woman 45 and players men 38 and 29, still gambling. They offer a game; what you stake matters.

## Questions with defaults
1. Share: about 4 trouble ships (4, 7, 10, 13), 3 grabby or suggestive (6, 11, 14), 7 harmless, 2 ghosts. Default yes; East is the seediest region so it carries more.
2. Scalekin: only the salt barge has them. Default yes.
3. Ship names: I give each a distinct working name when I build. Default yes.
