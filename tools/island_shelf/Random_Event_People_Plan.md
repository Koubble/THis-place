# Random Event People Plan (draft for corrections)
35 people with no home island. They can turn up on any island, on any ship or in a travel event.
Appearance chance: starts at 2% per mission or event, rises 1 point for each one without an appearance, caps at 10%, resets to 2% when one appears.
All adults (18 or over). All human except the tide-brute (ogre) and the dock-matron (Amazon). No weapons in image prompts. Where a person can meet the party: the pool is drawn at random; a "where" tag below limits a few.
Prompts: "1: nude" for the six single-image menacing people; "2: clothed, nude" for most; "3: clothed, nude, swim/underwear" for fans and castaways (they can appear at beaches).
